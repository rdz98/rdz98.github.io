## About Me

I am a researcher jointly affiliated with Zhejiang University and NANHU Brain-Computer Interface Institute. My research focuses on Brain-Computer Interfaces, particularly invasive neural encoding and decoding. My long-term goal is to advance our understanding of neural information processing and translate these insights into the next generation of intelligent brain-computer interfaces. I am especially interested in developing computational approaches that connect advances in artificial intelligence with neural engineering and neurotechnology.

I welcome opportunities to collaborate with researchers and students who share similar interests. If our research directions overlap, I would be happy to connect and explore potential collaborations.

## Research Interests

- **Brain-Computer Interfaces:** invasive neural encoding & decoding
- **Trustworthy AI:** privacy-preserving computation, backdoor attacks
